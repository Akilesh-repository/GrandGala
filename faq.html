24/7 Support

Always Available

For weddings and large corporate galas, we strongly recommend booking 12 to 18 months in advance to secure your preferred date, especially during peak seasons (Spring and early Fall). For smaller gatherings, 3 to 6 months is typically sufficient, though availability is not guaranteed.

To officially secure your date, we require a non-refundable deposit of 25% of the total estimated package cost. The remaining balance is divided into scheduled payments, with the final payment due 30 days prior to your event.

Our largest space, The Crystal Ballroom, can comfortably accommodate up to 500 guests for a seated dinner with a dance floor. For a standing cocktail reception, the capacity expands to 700 guests. We also have smaller rooms for intimate parties of 50-150 guests.

Yes! We have a private, secure parking lot that can accommodate over 300 vehicles. Additionally, complimentary valet parking is included with our Gold and Platinum packages, and can be added as an a la carte service for any other event.

GrandGala prides itself on our world-class in-house executive culinary team, so all food and beverage services must be provided by us. However, we do make exceptions for specialty cultural cuisines or specific religious dietary requirements (e.g., Glatt Kosher), subject to an outside catering fee and pre-approval of the vendor.

Absolutely. During your menu tasting and planning sessions, you can specify any dietary restrictions (vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, nut allergies, etc.). Our chefs will prepare customized, equally delicious alternatives for those specific guests at no additional charge.

If you cancel more than 180 days before your event, you forfeit only the initial 25% deposit. Cancellations within 180 days require 50% of the total estimated cost, and within 60 days, the full balance is required. If you need to reschedule, we will transfer your deposit to a new date within 12 months, subject to availability and a rescheduling fee.

We welcome you to customize the space to your liking! However, to preserve our historic venue, we do not allow nails, staples, or tape on the walls. Confetti, loose glitter, and unprotected open flames are also strictly prohibited. Enclosed candles (hurricanes or votives) are perfectly fine.
